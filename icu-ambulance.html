24/7 ICU Ambulance Service in Hyderabad

Advanced Life Support (ALS) ICU Ambulance equipped with mobile intensive care capabilities for critical patients requiring continuous medical surveillance.

Hospital-Grade Intensive Care on Wheels

When a critically ill patient requires transfer between medical facilities or immediate emergency retrieval from home, a standard transport ambulance is not sufficient. MN Ambulance Service Hyderabad operates dedicated Advanced Life Support (ALS) ICU Ambulances configured to mirror a hospital intensive care unit.

Equipment Inside Our ICU Ambulance

  • Multi-parameter Patient Monitor: Continuous real-time tracking of ECG, SpO2, NIBP, pulse rate, temperature, and respiration.
  • Advanced Transport Ventilator (AVIA TransVent 2.0): Capable of invasive and non-invasive positive pressure ventilation with PEEP controls.
  • Biphasic Defibrillator & External Pacemaker: For rapid cardiac intervention and rhythm management.
  • Syringe & Volumetric Infusion Pumps: Accurate administration of inotropic and life-sustaining intravenous medications.
  • Electric & Manual Suction Apparatus: Keeping airways completely clear during intubation or transit.
  • Large Bulk Oxygen Cylinders: Multi-tank manifold system ensuring uninterruptible oxygen flow.
  • Emergency Resuscitation Drug Kit: Adrenaline, atropine, amiodarone, sedatives, and emergency IV fluids.

When is an ICU Ambulance Required?

An ICU ambulance is recommended in situations such as:

  • Patients on ventilator or non-invasive BiPAP/CPAP support.
  • Post-cardiac arrest or severe cardiogenic shock.
  • Major stroke with altered consciousness or airway compromise.
  • Severe polytrauma, head injuries, or multi-organ failure.
  • Inter-hospital transfers of ICU-admitted patients for advanced surgery or ECMO.
