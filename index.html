About MN Ambulance Service Hyderabad

MN Ambulance Service Hyderabad provides 24/7 emergency medical transportation and non-emergency patient transfer services across Hyderabad, Secunderabad, and surrounding areas in Telangana. With our modern fleet of Advanced Life Support (ALS) and Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances, we deliver hospital-grade critical care on wheels.

Our vehicles are equipped with state-of-the-art portable ventilators (AVIA TransVent 2.0), multipara monitors, oxygen supply systems, cardiac defibrillators, and neonatal incubators operated by experienced, certified paramedic teams.
